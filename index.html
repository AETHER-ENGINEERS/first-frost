First Frost

Twilight, leaves turning, a little smoke on the air. Save the garden together before the hard frost.
Waiting to begin
Frost creeps in from the west (left), one column each round: a column first gets a light frost, then a hard one. Tender plants (🍅 🌿 🫘 🎃 🌻) die at the first frost; hardy ones (🥬 🧅 🥕 🍃) survive a light frost but not a hard one. Covered plants live through anything. Pumpkins can only be harvested from round 4, sunflowers (3 points) from round 5. Each of you acts once a round, in any order; the frost moves once everyone has acted. You carry one covering at a time; a cloche covers one plant, a fleece a 2×2 patch, the greenhouse a whole column.
